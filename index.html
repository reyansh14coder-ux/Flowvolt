02 — How it works

Four steps.
Two minutes.

01

Clip on

The lazy-stand-style extendable grip slides to fit any pipe from 26 to 42 mm. No tools, no shutdown.

02

Listen

Two ultrasonic transducers fire a pulse through the water and time its echo. Leaks change the sound signature.

03

Decide

Rules R1–R7 compare night flow against a 96-band baseline. Persistence filters noise from a real leak.

04

Alert

NB-IoT pushes the verdict to the cloud — no Wi-Fi at the pipe. Never a silent failure: every message carries a confidence.
